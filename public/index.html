Why Boba Lab exists

Boba Lab is a personal project about taste. I love boba, and I am curious why one person wants a fully sweet brown sugar milk tea while another wants unsweetened jasmine with grass jelly.

Boba is enjoyed in many places and in many ways. When you answer, you add your cup to the picture, from wherever you are.

The survey asks for no names or emails. The one place-related question is optional and broad: a part of the world such as "Europe" or "Central Asia and Caucasus", never a country or a city. The site saves only totals, like how many people picked chewy textures, so no one's individual answers exist anywhere. Your language choice is remembered only on your own device.

The results come from people who choose to answer, so treat them as a conversation, not a study. The more people take part, the more interesting the picture gets.
